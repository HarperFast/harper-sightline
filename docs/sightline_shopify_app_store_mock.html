App description

Sightline gives Shopify teams a visual black box recorder for commerce operations. Instead of reading raw success and failure rows, teams can see order, inventory, fulfillment, product, customer, return, and refund events as timelines and flow maps.

When a destination breaks, Sightline shows the affected records and lets operators replay eligible events without reprocessing the entire store.

Works best for

  • Shopify Plus and growing merchants with fulfillment, inventory, ERP, or marketplace dependencies.
  • Agencies and systems integrators managing multiple stores.
  • Commerce teams that need incident recovery, audit history, and operational visibility.
